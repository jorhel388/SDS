Customer Management non-trend walkthrough

Walkthrough:

The Analysis Reference Date is 15th August 2010. The Analysis Period was set to ONE QUARTER. (This means that the CMRP is also one quarter.) So the last complete quarter prior to 15th August is used as the Analysis Period (April - June 2010 i.e. 2010 Q2).

The Outcome Length was defined as "3" - so, granted that the CMRP is one quarter, this translates to three quarters. So the "strict" Outcome Period is three quarters after the Analysis Period i.e. 2011 Q1.

Consider four accounts ("A", "B", "C" and "D") that have updates as per the table above.

Account "C" has no update in the Analysis Period, and will be excluded from the report. Account "D" has no update in (even) the generalised Outcome Period, and will also be excluded from the report (assuming outcome measures are used!).

For "Outcome" measures, the strict Outcome Period is used, so, for any such measures, Account "B" would also be excluded. Account "A" would be included, and the update shown in green (Analysis Period) would be used for the slicing, segmentation, inclusion filter and exclusion filter information. The account shown in red (Outcome Period) would be used for the measure and measure filter information.

For "Outcome - Closed" and "Outcome - Written off" measures, both accounts "A" and "B" would be considered. Their outcome updates (the ones in red) would be checked to see if there was a close date or write-off date set. (If not, it is assumed that the account not closed/written off, and the account would be excluded.) If so, the measure value from the outcome update would be aggregated (i.e. counted, summed, etc).

For "Outcome - Open" measures, both accounts "A" and "B" would be considered. However, in this case, the accounts would be excluded if their outcome updated HAD a closed date or write-off date.
