Acquisitions: Additional concepts

Walkthrough:

The Analysis Reference Date is 15th August 2010. The Analysis Period was set to FIVE MONTHS. So the five complete months prior to 15th August are used as the Analysis Period (Mar - Jul 2010). Any APPLICATION whose system decision date is not in this five month windows would be excluded from the report. Any application that did not subsequently get a record created on an (the) update table is going to be excluded. Assume that the Outcome Length was defined as "3" - so, granted that the CMRP is one quarter, this translates to three quarters. So the "strict" Outcome Period is three quarters after the Analysis Period.

Consider four Accounts ("A", "B", "C" and "D") that have updates as per the table above. (The values in the Analysis Period (green bar) and Account Opening period (blue bar) indicate when each account was decisioned and opened.)

Account "A" was decisioned in March 2010, but the account was only opened in October 2010. October 2010 falls into the 2010 Q4 CMRP. As such, the outcome update for this account would be the latest update in 2011 Q3 (three quarters later). As this is an Acquisition report, the Analysis Period information would not be taken from any Customer Management update, but from the Acquisition information (a one-time event per application).

Account "B" was decisioned a bit later (May 2010), but the account was opened faster (August 2010, which falls in the 2010 Q3 CMRP). So the outcome information for Account B would be from June 2011 (the latest update in the 2011 Q2 CMRP). So we find that the information for Account A and B are coming from different points in time, because their accounts were opened at different points in time, and thus different windows are needed to ensure a consistent interval between the account's opening, and the behaviour being observed. (If we consider measures on bad rates, utilisation etc, this standardisation is essential.)

Like Account "B", Account "C" had its account opened in the 2010 Q3 CMRP. However, as there is no update for Account "C" in (or before) 2011 Q2, this account would be excluded (as there is no outcome information available).

For insufficient/sufficient outcome measures, an additional concept is necessary - the most recent update date. As per the example above, imagine that the most recent update to the database was done on 30 Sept 2011 (perhaps because that is the current date). Account "D" (whose open date in Jan 2011 falls into the 2011 Q1 CMRP) would have its outcome period as 2011 Q4 - however, there is no data for that (perhaps because that is in the future). The Insufficient Outcome measure is there to catch Account "D" - the accounts that were decisioned in the Analysis Period, but whose Outcome Periods are beyond the data available AS A WHOLE (not just for that account).

"Outcome - Closed (Sufficient Outcome)" and "Outcome - Written Off (Sufficient Outcome)" work on the same principle inverted - they check that any application (that was decisioned during the Analysis Period) had its account opened in time that it is POSSIBLE for the outcome information to be available (whether or not it is - that would depend on the specific account), and whether the account was closed (or written off).
